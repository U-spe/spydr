Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Information stored on your device

SPYDR stores preferences such as theme, background, shortcuts, and tab appearance in your browser's local storage. These settings stay in that browser unless you clear them or export a backup.

AI features

When you use SPYDR's AI features, the message and any image you choose to send are sent through SPYDR's AI service to its configured model provider so a response can be generated. If you turn on screen sharing, your browser asks for permission. While sharing is active, SPYDR can capture a frame when you send a message; that frame is sent as part of the request. You can stop sharing using the controls in the AI page or your browser's sharing indicator.

Third-party services

SPYDR may load libraries, fonts, icons, hosting, and AI services from third-party providers. Those providers may receive standard connection information when your browser requests their resources. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Data retention and choices

SPYDR does not provide a user account or a central settings profile on this page. You can clear local browser storage to remove locally saved preferences. Messages and media sent to AI providers are handled under the provider's applicable terms and retention settings.

Children and contact

Do not submit personal, confidential, or sensitive information to AI features. Questions about this policy can be sent to help.solargames@gmail.com.
