About

Snaker io

A fast-paced multiplayer snake game where you eat, grow, boost, and compete against AI bots. Survive as long as you can while the game speeds up!

Developed by

— Free online games, no downloads needed.

How to Play

Move your mouse to steer. Hold SPACE, SHIFT, or click to boost (costs length). Eat food to grow. Avoid running into other snakes or yourself. Kill bots by making them crash into your body.

Upgrades

Size — Start each round with more segments.
Boost — More boost capacity and faster regeneration.
Magnet — Pull nearby food toward you automatically.

Tips

• Coil around smaller bots to trap them.
• Use boost to escape dangerous situations.
• Eating dead snake food gives you a big size boost.
• The game speeds up every 20 seconds — stay alert!
