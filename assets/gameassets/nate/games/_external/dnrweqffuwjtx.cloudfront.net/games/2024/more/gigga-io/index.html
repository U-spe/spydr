About game
gigga.io is a fan-made sequel to the game Diep.io. At the start of the game the player controls a simple Tank, called the Basic, and defeats the Polygons and other players to level up and upgrade their tank to the next level and unlock up special points to increase their health, damage, speed, body damage, bullet damage, etc.
